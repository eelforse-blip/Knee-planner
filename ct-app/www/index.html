Keyboard (PC)

Wheel or ↑ ↓ – slices · Ctrl + wheel – zoom · right-drag – window · middle-drag – pan · PgUp / PgDn – 10 slices · 1–9, 0 – presets · A / C / S – axial, coronal, sagittal · M – MIP · I – invert · Space – cine · F – fit · R – reset · Delete – undo measurement. Drag files or a ZIP onto the window.

Privacy

Images are opened on this device only and are never uploaded. Closing the study removes them from memory.

For viewing and teaching. Not a certified diagnostic workstation: reports must be made on approved PACS / diagnostic monitors.
